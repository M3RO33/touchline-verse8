CHARACTERS
Quaternius: Universal Base Characters, Standard (free) version.
Original asset: Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf, with its body, eyes and eyebrows.
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The original license is preserved in licenses/LICENSE-Quaternius-characters.txt.

Textures: T_Superhero_Male_Dark, T_Eye_Brown and T_Hair_1_BaseColor, from the
same CC0 character pack. Resized/encoded for the game. A skin tone variant,
short-hair texture band and team uniforms were authored by TOUCHLINE.

Changes to the CC0 character by TOUCHLINE:
- Slimmed superhero limb/chest mass to adult football-player proportions.
- Retargeted the native T-pose weights to the game's 42-controller skeleton.
- Corrected palm/finger rest-plane alignment and thumb placement.
- Tailored a football shirt and shorts from the weighted native surface.
- Authored continuous cloth torso topology, removing anatomical navel folds.
- Added fitted short hair, team jerseys, kit UVs, palette and collar openings.
- Corrected shorts pelvis/thigh weights; removed permanently hidden body faces.
- Simplified distant geometry while preserving shared buffers and skeletons.
- Added deterministic garment/turf contact correction during falls.

The football boots are original TOUCHLINE procedural geometry, reshaped with
slimmer uppers/soles, shorter heels and smaller team-colour panels. Their front
contact surface is retained to keep the visible ball contact and VAR consistent.
No MakeHuman anatomy, clothing, skin, eyes or hair are shipped in this version.

ANIMATION REFERENCES USED IN THE RUNTIME
Quaternius: Universal Animation Library 1, Standard, CC0 1.0.
Source clips: Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop and Sprint_Loop.
Source world-joint trajectories were converted into deterministic body/arm
curves. Football foot planting and ball-contact inverse kinematics remain in
TOUCHLINE's solver. This is retargeted animation data, not a claim that all
football motions are captured performances.
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database:
CMU subject 10, trial 03 (soccer kick), retargeted torso and arm timing curves.
The database permits use, modification and redistribution; the data itself
must not be resold. See the full distribution notice in the license records.
https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php

Tackles, reactions, gestures, foot/ball trajectories and rewind reconstruction
also use original TOUCHLINE procedural animation and authored contact timing.
