CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database — distribution record

Source: https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/ and https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php
The runtime uses a short retargeted derivative of subject 10, trial 03
(soccer kick), source seconds 0.75–1.60 at 120 Hz.

The database's published permission allows copying, modification and
redistribution of the motion capture data, including use in commercial
products and games. Reselling the data itself is prohibited, including
converted forms. This is a custom permission; it is not a CC0 dedication.

Credit: The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

TOUCHLINE changes: short crop, five-tap smoothing, normalized heading,
separated horizontal root movement, right/left controller order, retargeted
upper-body and arm curves, cubic interpolation and contact-constrained
blending. The game does not include an observed ball-contact label; the
strike candidate is inferred from foot velocity. Football support and
ball-contact positions are generated by the game's IK.

No full raw capture archive is included. Consult the source FAQ for the
original permission statement.
